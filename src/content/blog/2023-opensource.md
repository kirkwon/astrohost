## AI for Everyone

While large language models (LLMs) have been making headlines for a few years, 2023 has been marked by a significant shift towards **open-source LLMs**. This trend is democratizing access to powerful AI technology, allowing researchers, developers, and enthusiasts to build upon and innovate with these models.

### The Open-Source Revolution

Projects like LLaMA and Alpaca, released by Meta AI and Stanford respectively, have provided the community with powerful base models. This has led to a flurry of activity, with researchers and developers fine-tuning these models for specific tasks, creating smaller, more efficient models, and exploring novel applications.

The open-source LLM movement is accelerating the pace of innovation in AI, and it is likely to have a profound impact on the future of the field. It is putting powerful AI tools in the hands of more people, and it is fostering a more collaborative and transparent research community.
