## The Shift from Chatbots to Agents

In 2025, the AI landscape fundamentally shifted from conversational interfaces to **agentic workflows**. While Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4 and Claude 3 previously acted as smart encyclopedias or coding assistants, the new paradigm introduced models capable of planning, executing, and evaluating complex, multi-step tasks autonomously.

This year marked the widespread adoption of AI agents that could browse the web, use software APIs, interact with databases, and even spawn sub-agents to parallelize work.

### Key Developments

- **Autonomous Tool Use:** Models became exceptionally proficient at recognizing when and how to use external tools (calculators, IDEs, browser automation) without explicit human prompting for each step.
- **Self-Correction and Reflection:** A major breakthrough was the ability of agents to evaluate their own output, recognize errors during execution, and course-correct before presenting a final result.
- **Multi-Agent Systems:** Frameworks allowing specialized AI agents to collaborate—such as a "Researcher Agent" handing off data to an "Analyst Agent," which then passes insights to a "Writer Agent"—became the standard for complex enterprise tasks.
