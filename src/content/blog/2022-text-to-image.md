## The Art of the Possible

In 2022, a new class of generative AI models emerged that captured the public imagination: **text-to-image models**. These models, such as OpenAI's **DALL-E 2** and **Midjourney**, can generate stunningly realistic and creative images from a simple text description.

### A New Creative Tool

Text-to-image models are a major breakthrough in the field of artificial intelligence, and they have opened up a whole new world of creative possibilities. They are being used by artists, designers, and advertisers to create everything from fine art to product mockups. They are also being used by researchers to explore the nature of creativity and to develop new ways of interacting with computers.
