## The Go Challenge

For decades, the ancient game of Go was considered the ultimate challenge for artificial intelligence. The game is vastly more complex than chess, with a branching factor that is far too large for traditional tree search algorithms. But in 2016, a computer program called **AlphaGo**, developed by Google DeepMind, achieved what many thought was impossible: it defeated Lee Sedol, the world champion Go player, in a five-game match.

### A New Kind of AI

AlphaGo's victory was a stunning demonstration of the power of **deep reinforcement learning**. The program learned to play Go by playing millions of games against itself, and it used a deep neural network to evaluate the board positions and select the best moves. AlphaGo's success was a major milestone in the history of artificial intelligence, and it has inspired a new wave of research in the field of deep reinforcement learning.
