## The Hardware Revolution

In 2011, the deep learning community increasingly recognized the game-changing potential of **Graphics Processing Units (GPUs)** for training neural networks. Originally designed for rendering complex graphics in video games, GPUs, with their thousands of parallel cores, proved to be the perfect tool for the massive matrix and vector computations required by deep learning algorithms.

### A Perfect Match

Training deep neural networks involves performing the same mathematical operations across vast amounts of data. This is precisely the kind of parallel workload that GPUs are designed to handle. By harnessing the power of GPUs, researchers were able to train their models in a fraction of the time it would have taken with traditional CPUs, making it possible to experiment with much larger and more complex network architectures.
