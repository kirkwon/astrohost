## Beyond Text

The latest wave of innovation in artificial intelligence is all about **multimodality**. AI models are no longer limited to processing and generating text. They can now understand and respond to a variety of different inputs, including images, audio, and video.

### A More Human-like AI

This is a major step towards creating more human-like AI systems. Humans experience the world through a variety of different senses, and we are able to seamlessly integrate information from all of these senses to form a coherent understanding of the world around us. Multimodal AI systems are beginning to do the same, and this is opening up a whole new range of possibilities.

We are already seeing the first generation of multimodal AI systems, such as Google's Gemini, and they are already capable of some amazing things. As these systems continue to develop, they are likely to have a profound impact on our lives.
