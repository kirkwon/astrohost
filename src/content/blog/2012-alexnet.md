## A Watershed Moment for Computer Vision

If there was one moment that catapulted deep learning into the mainstream, it was the 2012 **ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC)**. A deep convolutional neural network (CNN) called **AlexNet**, developed by Alex Krizhevsky, Ilya Sutskever, and Geoffrey Hinton, achieved a stunning victory, outperforming all previous methods by a massive margin.

### The AlexNet Architecture

AlexNet's success was due to a combination of factors. It was a deep network, with 8 layers, which allowed it to learn a hierarchical representation of features. It made use of new techniques like the ReLU activation function and dropout for regularization, which helped to prevent overfitting. And, crucially, it was trained on GPUs, which made it possible to train such a large and complex model.

AlexNet's victory was a resounding validation of the deep learning approach. It sparked a revolution in computer vision and led to a wave of new research and applications in the field.
