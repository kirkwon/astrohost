## The Blurring Lines of General Intelligence

In 2026, the definition of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) became the most hotly debated topic in science and philosophy. AI systems released this year demonstrated capabilities that challenged the traditional boundaries between specialized machine learning and generalized, human-like cognition.

These systems didn't just excel at known benchmarks; they demonstrated the ability to rapidly adapt to completely novel, unseen problems in physics, mathematics, and complex strategic planning with zero-shot learning.

### Breakthroughs in Reasoning

- **Continuous Learning architectures:** Unlike previous models that were frozen after training, the 2026 architectures introduced stable, continuous learning. They could update their understanding of the world dynamically without catastrophic forgetting.
- **Embodied AI:** The physical integration of highly capable reasoning models into humanoid and quadrupedal robots reached commercial viability. These embodied agents could navigate unstructured human environments, perform dexterous manipulation, and understand physical affordances simply by observing humans.
- **Scientific Discovery:** AI systems made autonomous, verifiable discoveries in materials science and synthetic biology, designing new protein structures and battery materials entirely from first principles reasoning.
