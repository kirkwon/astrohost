## The Dawn of Generative AI

In 2014, Ian Goodfellow and his colleagues at the University of Montreal introduced a novel and powerful new framework for generative modeling called **Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)**. This groundbreaking paper sparked a revolution in the field of artificial intelligence, paving the way for the incredible generative AI systems we see today.

### The Adversarial Game

A GAN consists of two neural networks, a **generator** and a **discriminator**, that are locked in a game of cat and mouse. The generator's goal is to create realistic-looking data, such as images or text, while the discriminator's goal is to distinguish between real data and the fake data created by the generator. The two networks are trained together, and over time, the generator becomes better and better at creating realistic data, while the discriminator becomes better and better at detecting fakes.

GANs have been used to generate stunningly realistic images, music, and text, and they have also found applications in areas such as drug discovery and materials science.
