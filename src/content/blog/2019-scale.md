## The Bigger the Better

By 2019, the success of pre-trained language models like BERT had led to an arms race in the field of natural language processing. Researchers began to train ever-larger language models, with billions and even trillions of parameters. The thinking was that bigger models, trained on more data, would be able to achieve better performance on a wider range of tasks.

### The Rise of the Foundation Model

This trend has led to the emergence of a new class of models, which are often referred to as **foundation models**. These are massive, pre-trained models that can be adapted to a wide range of downstream tasks. The development of foundation models has been a major focus of research in recent years, and it is likely to have a profound impact on the future of artificial intelligence.
