## A New Architecture for NLP

In 2017, a team of researchers at Google introduced a new neural network architecture called the **Transformer**. The Transformer was a major breakthrough in the field of natural language processing, and it has become the dominant architecture for a wide range of NLP tasks.

### The Power of Attention

The key innovation of the Transformer is the **attention mechanism**, which allows the network to weigh the importance of different words in the input sequence when it is making predictions. This allows the network to capture long-range dependencies between words, which is a major challenge for traditional recurrent neural networks.

The Transformer has been used to achieve state-of-the-art results on a wide range of NLP tasks, including machine translation, text summarization, and question answering. It is also the foundation for the large language models, like the one you are interacting with now.
