## Giving Words Meaning

While deep learning was making waves in computer vision, a quieter but equally profound revolution was taking place in the world of **natural language processing (NLP)**. In 2013, a team of researchers at Google, led by Tomas Mikolov, introduced a new model called **Word2Vec**, which fundamentally changed the way we represent and understand language.

### The Power of Embeddings

Word2Vec is a simple but powerful neural network model that learns to represent words as dense vectors, or **embeddings**. These embeddings capture the semantic relationships between words, so that words with similar meanings are located close to each other in the vector space. For example, the model learns that the vector for "king" is close to the vector for "queen", and that the relationship between "king" and "queen" is similar to the relationship between "man" and "woman".

This ability to capture the meaning of words was a major breakthrough for NLP. Word embeddings have become a fundamental building block for a wide range of NLP tasks, from machine translation to sentiment analysis.
