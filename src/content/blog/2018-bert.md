## The Power of Pre-training

Building on the success of the Transformer architecture, 2018 saw the emergence of a new paradigm in natural language processing: **pre-trained language models**. The key idea behind this approach is to train a large language model on a massive amount of text data, and then fine-tune the model on a specific downstream task.

### BERT: A Game Changer

The most influential of these pre-trained language models was **BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers)**, which was introduced by a team of researchers at Google. BERT is a deep, bidirectional Transformer model that is pre-trained on a massive amount of text data. It can then be fine-tuned for a wide range of NLP tasks, and it has achieved state-of-the-art results on many of them.

The success of BERT has led to a Cambrian explosion of new pre-trained language models, and they have become an essential tool for any NLP practitioner.
