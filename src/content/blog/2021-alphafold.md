## A Grand Challenge in Biology

The **protein folding problem** is one of the biggest challenges in biology. Proteins are the workhorses of the cell, and they perform a vast array of tasks. The function of a protein is determined by its three-dimensional shape, but predicting the shape of a protein from its amino acid sequence is an incredibly difficult problem.

### A Solution from DeepMind

In 2021, DeepMind announced that it had solved the protein folding problem with **AlphaFold 2**, a deep learning system that can predict the shape of a protein with unprecedented accuracy. AlphaFold 2 is a major breakthrough in the field of biology, and it is likely to have a profound impact on our understanding of disease and our ability to develop new drugs.
